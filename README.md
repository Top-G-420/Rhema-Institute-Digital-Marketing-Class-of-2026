# Rhema Institute – Digital Marketing Class of 2026

Welcome to the **Rhema Institute Digital Marketing Class of 2026** repository. This repository contains course materials, practical exercises, projects, and resources developed throughout the digital marketing program.

The goal of this program is to equip students with **practical digital marketing skills**, including website development, search engine optimization, online advertising, content creation, and e-commerce strategies.

---

## Course Outline

### 1. Marketing Fundamentals & Consumer Behaviour

Introduction to core marketing concepts, market segmentation, targeting, positioning, and understanding how consumers make purchasing decisions.

### 2. Digital Literacy

Basic digital skills required for modern marketing including online tools, internet platforms, productivity tools, and digital communication.

### 3. Website Fundamentals & WordPress Basics

Understanding how websites work and learning how to build and manage websites using WordPress.

Topics include:

* Domain names and hosting
* Website structure
* WordPress dashboard
* Themes and plugins
* Basic website customization

### 4. Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

Techniques used to improve website visibility on search engines.

Topics include:

* Keyword research
* On-page SEO
* Technical SEO basics
* SEO tools and analytics

### 5. Search Engine Marketing (SEM)

Introduction to paid search marketing and online advertising using search engines.

Topics include:

* Google Ads fundamentals
* Keyword targeting
* Ad creation
* Campaign optimization

### 6. Social Media Marketing (SMM)

Using social media platforms to promote brands, products, and services.

Topics include:

* Social media strategy
* Content planning
* Audience engagement
* Social media analytics

### 7. Content Marketing

Creating valuable content to attract and retain audiences.

Topics include:

* Content strategy
* Blogging
* Visual content
* Storytelling and brand messaging

### 8. Email & Mobile Marketing

Using email campaigns and mobile marketing strategies to communicate with customers.

Topics include:

* Email campaign creation
* Marketing automation
* Mobile-friendly marketing strategies
* Performance tracking

### 9. E-Commerce

Understanding how online businesses operate and how to market products online.

Topics include:

* E-commerce platforms
* Product listings
* Online payment systems
* Customer experience

### 10. Final Project

Students apply the skills learned throughout the course to build and present a **complete digital marketing strategy or project**, which may include:

* Website creation
* SEO optimization
* Social media campaigns
* Content marketing strategy
* Digital advertising plan
